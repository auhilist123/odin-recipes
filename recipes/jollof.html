Jollof Rice

Home

Description

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions and a blend of spices. It is known for its rich flavour, vibrant colour and delicious taste.

This dish is often served with fried chicken, plantain or salad and is a favourite at parties, celebrations and everyday meals.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long grain rice
  • 4 medium tomatoes (blended)
  • 2–3 red bell peppers (blended)
  • 1 large onion (chopped)
  • 3 cloves garlic (chopped)
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2–3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken stock or water
  • Seasoning (salt, curry powder, thyme, paprika, etc.)

Steps

  1. Wash the rice and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers and 1 onion.
  3. Heat oil in a pot, add the chopped onion and fry until soft.
  4. Add the blended mixture and tomato paste, then cook for a few minutes.
  5. Add the rice, seasoning and chicken stock. Stir and bring to a boil.
  6. Reduce heat, cover and cook until the rice is tender (about 20–25 minutes).
  7. Serve hot with fried chicken, plantain or salad.
