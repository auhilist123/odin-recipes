Description

Beans are a nutritious and versatile ingredient that can be used in a variety of dishes. They are a good source of protein, fiber, and essential nutrients. Beans can be cooked in soups, stews, salads, or as a side dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dried beans
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Rinse the beans and soak them in water for at least 4 hours.
  2. Drain the beans and place them in a pot with fresh water.
  3. Add the onion, garlic, and spices to the pot.
  4. Cook the beans until they are tender, about 1-2 hours.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Add Palm-oil if desired.
  7. Serve the beans as a side dish or incorporate them into your favorite recipes.
